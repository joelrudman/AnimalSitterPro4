## Description (1,870 of 4,000)
Never blank on a bartender's name again.

Pourtrait remembers the people behind the bar at the pubs you visit. Save a bartender's name, a photo and a few notes, and Pourtrait files them under the right pub automatically. Next time you walk in, you'll know exactly who's who.

KNOWS WHICH PUB YOU'RE IN
Pourtrait uses your location and Apple Maps to work out the pub you're in, so every bartender is saved in the right place. Wrong guess? Pick from nearby pubs, search by name, or type the pub in yourself.

NAME, PHOTO AND NOTES
Take a photo or choose one from your library, then add whatever helps you remember: their signature drink, the quiz night they run, the team they support.

SEE WHO YOU KNOW AS YOU ARRIVE
Walk into a pub where you've saved bartenders and their names and photos appear in a Live Activity on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. If the app is closed, you'll get a notification instead.

YOUR WHOLE DIRECTORY
Every bartender you know, grouped by pub with the nearest first. Search by name, pub or notes, and star your favourites.

BACKED UP TO YOUR ICLOUD
Everything is saved to your private iCloud automatically. Get a new iPhone, sign in with the same Apple ID, and your bartenders and photos come back by themselves. It works offline too, and syncs when you're back in signal.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
There's no account to create. Your directory is stored on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. It isn't shared with other users, and it isn't uploaded to our servers.

FREE, WITH NO POP-UPS
Pourtrait is free and supported by one small banner, never pop-ups or video ads. A one-off purchase removes it for good.

Please ask before you take someone's photo, and drink responsibly.

Requires iOS 17 or later. Arrival alerts while the app is closed need "Always" location access. The Dynamic Island view needs an iPhone with the Dynamic Island.

## Notes for App Review
Pourtrait doesn't require an account or sign-in.

The app uses location to detect which pub the user is in. To test it away from a pub, tap "Not here? Change pub" on the Here tab, search for any pub or bar (for example "The Swan"), or enter a pub name by hand. Then tap + to add a bartender.

"Always" location is optional and only requested if the user turns on "Also alert me when the app is closed" in Settings → Arrival alerts. It is used for on-device region monitoring of pubs where the user has saved bartenders, to show a Live Activity or notification on arrival. Location is never sent to our servers.

All user data is stored on the device and in the user's private CloudKit database.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob, with Google's UMP consent form for UK/EEA users. The non-consumable in-app purchase "Remove Ads" (com.pourtrait.app.removeads) disables them, and Settings has a Restore Purchases button.
