How it works.

Walk in

Open Pourtrait and it works out which pub you're in.

Say hello

Add the bartender's name, a photo if they're happy with it, and a note to jog your memory.

Come back

Next time you visit, Pourtrait reminds you who's who before you reach the bar.

Your list stays yours.

The bartenders you save, their photos and your notes are stored on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud. They're never shared with other users or uploaded to our servers. Your location is used on your phone to find the pub you're in. The ad banner is provided by Google AdMob, which collects some data to show ads. The privacy policy explains what, and buying Remove Ads switches it off.

Questions.

Is Pourtrait free?

Yes. The app is free and supported by a single small banner advert. If you'd rather not see it, a one-off in-app purchase removes ads for good, on all your devices.

Can other people see the bartenders I save?

No. Your directory is private to you. It's stored in your own iCloud account, not in a shared database.

What happens if I get a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID and open Pourtrait. Your bartenders and photos are restored from iCloud by themselves.

Why does it ask for "Always" location?

Only if you want arrival alerts while the app is closed. It lets your iPhone notice when you walk into a pub where you've saved bartenders. You can say no and still use everything else.

What if it picks the wrong pub?

Tap "Not here? Change pub" to choose from nearby pubs, search by name, or type the pub in yourself.

Which iPhones does it work on?

Any iPhone running iOS 17 or later. The Dynamic Island view needs an iPhone 14 Pro or newer; other models show the Live Activity on the Lock Screen.
